NAFv4.1 p.80 states that its Information Model is conceptual and not intended for direct implementation. Accordingly, these adapters are a documented tool mapping. The separate native UAF constraints report remains applicable: ../uaf/COVERAGE.md.

NAFv4.1 p.91 labels the three C2 objectives Goal. The application type Goal extends the native UAF EnterpriseGoal and therefore inherits its attributes, relationship compatibility and C2 checks. EnterpriseGoal remains supported. This is an explicit source-vocabulary adapter, not a native UAF or ADMBw stereotype named Goal.

The C2 cut-out on p.90 explicitly permits desires from EnterprisePhase to Capability, EnterpriseVision and Goal. The adapters DesiresCapability, DesiresVision and DesiresGoal preserve these distinct endpoint types. Every planning phase requires at least one of these relationships; the p.91 example is accepted with DesiresVision and does not require an additional direct phase-to-capability link.

C3 permits specialization and grouping context without a CapabilityDependency at every displayed capability, following the SAR example on p.93. The application profile requires at least one dependency in the view; it cannot establish completeness against real-world dependencies.

## Review still required

- Structural application mapping in arch.js; not the complete UML/SysML semantics or an official NATO/OMG implementation guide.
- No certification or complete NAF/UAF conformance is claimed. All 47 IDs are covered structurally, not all normative semantics.
- The inherited UAF coverage report still applies, including deferred native constraints. Application adapters are identified in type help.
- Only included view elements/links count toward viewpoint connection rules. Typed references resolve in the model; designated structural references must also be visible.
- NAF methodology, stakeholder approval, full state/sequence execution semantics and qualitative completeness require human review. See profiles/naf41/COVERAGE.md.

- **C2:** EnterprisePhase is the required planning-phase representation in this mapping; ActualEnterprisePhase can additionally record actual states. The C2 cut-out permits desires from an enterprise phase to a capability, vision or goal. Every planning phase requires at least one of these links; a direct DesiresCapability is not mandatory when DesiresVision/DesiresGoal is present.
- **C3:** Not every displayed capability must have a CapabilityDependency: specialization/grouping context is allowed, as SAR demonstrates in the p.93 example. The view requires at least one dependency; completeness of relevant real-world dependencies cannot be checked automatically.
- **S5:** Transition guards, state ownership consistency, reachability and execution are not evaluated.
- **S6:** Order is a positive integer; UML interaction fragments, time semantics and unique sequence positions require review.
- **S7:** Use ServiceOperationParameter or ServiceFunctionParameter: the unqualified UAF ServiceParameter lacks the required owner/payload projection.
- **L2:** Needline source/target are references rendered in class details, not native UML AssociationClass ends. Structural hierarchy can use GroupsLogicalResource.
- **L2-L3:** Rich pictures/external illustrations and their interpretation require human review. This permissive concept view is intentionally not a substitute for formal viewpoints.
- **L3:** LogicalInteraction.conveyed is typed and nonempty; edge payloads may be shared with other views. UAF OperationalExchange may supplement, but not replace, the application interaction.
- **L5:** State-machine execution, same-owner transitions and guard completeness require review.
- **L6:** Interaction fragments, concurrency and sequence ordering consistency require review.
- **P1:** Resource/function taxonomy may consist of a single root; decomposition and classification completeness require review.
- **P3:** Protocol extends Standard, so a governing protocol also satisfies Standard membership. Matching the exact interaction protocol to both endpoint implementations requires review.
- **P5:** State-machine execution, same-owner transitions and guard completeness require review.
- **P6:** Interaction fragments, concurrency and sequence ordering consistency require review.
- **A1:** Glossary relevance and metadata completeness require review. Architecture descriptions and their native viewpoint references are optional here.
- **A2:** The native View.viewpoint reference and the drawn ConformsToViewpoint link must be kept consistent by the author.
- **A3:** The governing method is recorded as text; its domain-specific rule is not executed.
- **A4:** A non-tailored methodology can record an explicit no-extension decision and rationale. Process adherence requires review.
- **A6:** Succession is acyclic; version-label uniqueness, same-product succession and date ordering require review.
- **A7:** Whether measures are sufficient for an assessment and whether desired/achieved metrics are comparable require review.
- **A8:** Applicability, compliance with standard contents and organizational ratification require review.

## Use of ADMBw reference

The supplied MDG technology declares NAF 4.0 plus Bundeswehr extensions. Its 318 domain stereotypes were inspected as data; diagram/toolbox stereotypes are not treated as domain classes. No MDG scripts, shape scripts, templates or images are executed or copied into the runtime.

StrategicConstraint supports the additional concept. ServiceDependency is restricted to service-to-service in this application profile. ContainsView, ConformsToViewpoint, StakeholderHasConcern and FramesConcern use the directions shown by ViewsInArchitecturalDescription, CompliesViewpoint, StakeholderConcern and ConcernForViewpoint. These are named application mappings, not an import of the entire ADMBw.

The inspected MDG defines EnterpriseGoal (a PropertySet / SysML requirement applied to Class), not a separate Goal stereotype. A dedicated ADMBw profile would be appropriate for Bundeswehr-specific vocabulary and constraints; the Goal adapter alone does not require one.

**Important differences:** ADMBw Evaluates relates FulfilmentCriterion to FitCriterion; it must not be reused as a synonym for NAFv4.1 Measure → AssessmentCriterion. ADMBw ImplementsProtocol starts at ProtocolImplementation; ResourceImplementsProtocol is an explicitly documented direct resource projection. Neither is silently substituted.
